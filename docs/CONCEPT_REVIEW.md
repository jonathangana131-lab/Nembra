# Concept review log

## Generated concept pass 01 — REJECTED
A broad AI-generated concept board was produced during the visual phase. It is **not** an accepted product design and must not be copied into the app.

### Why it failed the product bar
- Used the invented brand **VELOX** instead of the researched working identity **Nembra**.
- Presented unverified BLE services (`FEE9`, `FEE8`) as if they were MAXSHOT facts.
- Claimed an estimated notification rate as a product fact.
- Displayed a gauge labeled `THROTTLE` without proof that the scooter exposes throttle position.
- Invented live voltage/power numbers and performance metrics that were not grounded in the physical unit.
- Leaned too heavily into dark neon/EV-game instrumentation rather than native iOS portrait hierarchy.
- Used too many bordered cards and gauges, conflicting with the desired restrained Liquid Glass/content-layer principles.

### Elements worth retaining only as high-level exploration
- Dedicated landscape composition instead of rotated portrait UI.
- Navigation sharing space with speed rather than replacing the dashboard.
- Multiple original vehicle angles as a future asset set.

### Required direction for next concept pass
- Nembra identity only.
- Portrait UI should look unmistakably native iOS 27, brighter/system-adaptive and much less “gaming dashboard.”
- No protocol UUIDs, packet rates, throttle labels, voltage/current/power, or performance values unless explicitly marked concept/simulated and already verified.
- Landscape cockpit may be darker/more instrument-like but must remain minimal, glanceable, and honest.
- Vehicle artwork must follow the real V1S Pro physical references without copying manufacturer renders.
