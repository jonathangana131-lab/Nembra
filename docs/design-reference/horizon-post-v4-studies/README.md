# Horizon post-V4 Drive composition studies

Status: **user-approved post-V4 composition direction; mandatory refinement; not pixel-final**

These studies were created on 2026-08-19 after the user rejected the Horizon V4 pixel execution. They are grounded in:

- `docs/design-reference/nembra-1.0-gold-glass/selected-home-gold-glass.png` for graphite/gold material language, energy lighting, typography hierarchy, and restrained functional Liquid Glass;
- `docs/design-reference/horizon-v4-final/NEMBRA_HORIZON_V4_PRODUCTION_HANDOFF.md` for requirements and telemetry behavior only; and
- the real iPhone 12 landscape app-content aspect ratio, `844 × 390` logical points.

The PNGs are ImageGen composition studies, not screenshots, shipped assets, physical-scooter evidence, or accepted UI. Do not rasterize them into the app. Rebuild the retained direction in native SwiftUI from real presentation state, then compare the exact Xcode 27/iPhone 12 runtime against the study.

The user selected the `Energy Chamber` family as the new direction on 2026-08-19. What is authoritative is its premium near-black/concrete tactility, fine restrained gold light, grounded shadow/reflection, quiet top chrome, giant centered speed, and spatially calm single-instrument composition. Its generated live-power gauge and typography are explicitly **not** accepted and must be redesigned before implementation can be called visually complete.

## Permanent interaction boundary

Every study uses one unified engineered battery instrument:

- percentage is the default and sole primary value;
- one tap replaces that value inside the same battery with accepted adaptive range;
- another tap returns to percentage;
- SOC fill always means charge;
- no simultaneous, detached, or duplicated primary range readout is permitted; and
- learning, unavailable, retained, and low-battery states remain truthful.
