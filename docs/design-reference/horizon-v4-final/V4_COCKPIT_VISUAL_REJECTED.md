# Horizon V4 cockpit visual rejection

Status: permanent rejection record, 2026-08-19

The user rejected the Horizon V4 Drive/Navigation/Explore PNGs as pixel-level production targets. They remain in this directory only as historical design evidence and must not be faithfully reproduced, incrementally polished, or treated as layout authority.

The rejection includes:

- the generic compact battery pill and detached terminal;
- simultaneous/duplicated percentage and range readouts;
- the giant generic navigation glass card;
- flat map blocks and an over-glowing route tube;
- a crowded bottom mega-pill and tiny segmented Drive/Nav/Explore control;
- isolated top controls, awkward spacing, and incomplete material/lighting hierarchy;
- any first runnable cockpit presented as a finished 1.0 design.

An additional rejected runtime screenshot supplied during the decision had SHA-256 `68740cdcc00df2168fa3b99baba441e63d5b5a7576fe8964478f98ef3103bd25`. It is rejection evidence only and is not required to ship in the app or to authorize telemetry claims.

What remains authoritative from V4 is the functional contract in `NEMBRA_HORIZON_V4_PRODUCTION_HANDOFF.md`: enormous readable speed, truthful responsive propulsion/power, automatic recording, durable Today/duration/odometer facts, a one-value battery toggle whose fill always means SOC, Drive/Navigation/Explore transformation, verified road coverage, reconnect/error/empty states, accessibility, motion discipline, and exact-head Xcode 27 performance evidence.

The next visual direction must be developed through several internal iPhone 12 landscape studies grounded in those requirements and the selected portrait Home design language. The strongest study must be refined with real Xcode 27 screenshots before it becomes any new visual authority.
