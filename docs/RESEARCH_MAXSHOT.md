# MAXSHOT V1S Pro research ledger

Date: 2026-08-04

## Physical design — publicly corroborated
Across official/retailer imagery and real-owner photographs, the V1S Pro family consistently presents:
- slim black/graphite folding stem and deck
- central handlebar display area
- 10-inch-class dark wheels with red accent details in common product photography
- front and rear suspension elements in the commonly marketed V1S Pro configuration
- rear fender over the back wheel
- front lighting integrated near the stem/front assembly
- compact folding commuter proportions rather than a wide dual-stem performance chassis

Owner imagery is especially valuable for cockpit/stem proportions because polished retailer renders can hide cable routing and production variation.

## Published functional claims — corroborated, but batch-sensitive
Manual/retailer material commonly lists:
- 4 modes: Walk / Eco / D / Sport
- app lock/unlock
- cruise
- speed limit adjustment
- headlight control
- speed/battery display
- Zero Start / Kick Start
- folding design
- solid/honeycomb tire marketing on the 10-inch configuration

## Spec conflict that the app must respect
Some public pages market `500 W`, `42 V 10.5 Ah`, and `22 mph`. The user's observed physical controller label is `36 V / 15 A / 350 W`. Therefore Nembra must not decide capability or protocol behavior from storefront wattage alone.

`MAXSHOT V1S Pro` is the human-readable vehicle identity. Diagnostics must retain raw hardware/firmware identifiers once captured so production batches can be distinguished safely.

## Visual asset truth tiers
### VERIFIED-enough for original concept artwork
- overall commuter geometry
- folding stem/deck silhouette
- central display/cockpit placement
- wheel/fender proportions
- graphite/black base with restrained red details commonly shown

### LIKELY / batch-sensitive
- exact suspension hardware shape
- exact rear-brake external appearance
- exact logo/decal placement
- exact red accent geometry

### UNKNOWN until owner-unit photo set / measurements
- charging-port placement on this exact batch
- underside/deck hardware
- exact controller compartment geometry
- exact display bezel dimensions
- exact tire tread/honeycomb sidewall pattern on the user's unit

## Artwork rule
Generate original Nembra vehicle art from the physical form, not traced/manufacturer promotional renders. Keep branding decals generic unless an owner photo verifies placement.
