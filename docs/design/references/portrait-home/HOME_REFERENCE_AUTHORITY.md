## Authority precedence

The preserved source uses blue energy accents. The repository's later selected
gold derivative at
`docs/design-reference/nembra-1.0-gold-glass/selected-home-gold-glass.png` and
the explicit Nembra 1.0 product direction supersede that hue. Production uses
deep black, cool graphite, warm white, cool secondary neutrals, healthy green,
and selective warm gold. The source remains authoritative for composition and
material character; the gold contract remains authoritative for color.

The reference does not authorize these pixels as product truth:

- `73%`, `8.4 mi`, daily distance, duration, mode, connection, and ride-history
  values are illustrative only;
- the pictured scooter cannot establish an ES80 identity, regional variant, or
  license to redistribute its raster;
- the route thumbnail is not accepted route evidence;
- a decorative treatment cannot promote stale, simulated, missing, or
  unverified telemetry.

Production values must continue to come from accepted app/domain contracts.
Unknown, stale, retained, simulator-only, and unavailable states remain visible
as such.

## Non-negotiable visual gate

Home is not visually accepted until an exact-head GitHub-hosted Xcode 27 run on
iPhone 12 / iOS 27 produces deterministic screenshots for the truthful state
matrix, and a same-viewport comparison against this source demonstrates:

- a dimensional engineered battery with truthful SOC-clipped fine ribbing,
  graphite copy well, restrained warm-gold depth, shell/rim, and terminal;
- no scooter intersection with percentage, range, availability, connection,
  warning, or accessibility content;
- believable tire/deck contact, floor plane, restrained reflection, and one
  integrated light scene;
- native Liquid Glass only on interactive controls, with sufficient contrast,
  depth, and Reduce Transparency behavior;
- native Dynamic Type reflow, VoiceOver truth, minimum targets, and native tab
  clearance at the unscrolled first fold.

Source tests and local Xcode 26 diagnostics cannot replace this comparison.
